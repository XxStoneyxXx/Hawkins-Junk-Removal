FAQ

How much does junk removal cost in Tulsa?

Price is based on the volume your items take on the truck. We give a free on-site quote before we start. We do not publish a price list — the driveway number is the real number.

Can you come today?

We work evenings and weekends, plus the next available window. We do not offer weekday daytime service, and we are not 24/7. If you request an evening or weekend window early enough that same day, we may be able to fit you in that window.

What do you take?

Furniture, appliances, construction debris, yard waste, hot tubs, and whole-space cleanouts. Most non-hazardous household and job-site junk. If you are unsure, send a photo with your quote.

What don’t you take?

Hazardous materials: wet paint and solvents, fuels, asbestos, pressurized tanks, and biohazard. If an item looks questionable, send a photo and we will tell you before we come out.

Do I have to move items to the curb?

No. Full-service from wherever it sits — inside, garage, attic, basement, or yard — unless access is unsafe.

What are your hours?

Monday–Friday 6–9pm. Saturday 8am–6pm. Sunday 10am–4pm. Evening and weekend windows only.

What happens to the junk?

We donate or recycle when we can and send the rest to proper disposal. Landfill is last, not first.

Do I need to be home?

Yes. Someone 18 or older needs to be there to approve the on-site quote before we load.
